Questions

Frequently Asked

Which areas do you serve?

We decorate events across Hyderabad and Secunderabad, at homes, apartments, community halls, banquet halls and offices.

How early should I book?

We recommend booking 1–2 weeks in advance, and earlier during wedding and festival seasons. For urgent dates, call us and we'll do our best.

Can you customise the theme and colours?

Yes. Share your ideas, colours or reference photos and we'll design a setup around them, including custom backdrops with names.

Do you use fresh or artificial flowers?

Both are available. Fresh-flower toranams and garlands are our speciality, and artificial options can suit longer events or tighter budgets.

How do I get a quote?

Call or WhatsApp +91 94921 74447 or +91 90003 45576, or use the enquiry form below. Tell us the event, date, venue and guest count and we'll send options.
